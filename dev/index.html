CFBoxes.CFBoxes — Module
CFBoxes

Rectangular boxes for finite-volume models. Each discretization is a submodule, whose exported names CFBoxes re-exports:

  • CGrid: 2D and 3D boxes on a C-grid, with halos, operators, wall boundary conditions and vortex-force schemes.

CFBoxes.CGrid — Module
CGrid

Rectangular 2D and 3D boxes for finite-volume models on a C-grid: grids with halos, the interpolation and difference operators, wall boundary conditions, and schemes for the vortex force ω×u.

Stress(τ)

Prescribed tangential stress τ = μ ∂uₜ/∂n [N m⁻²] exerted by the wall on the fluid, with n the outward normal and μ the dynamic viscosity, which must be nonzero. Components and values as for TangentialVelocity.

CFBoxes.CGrid.TangentialVelocity — Type
TangentialVelocity(U)

Prescribed tangential velocity U [m s⁻¹] of a moving wall. In 2D, U is along the wall: +x on the bottom and top walls, +z on the left and right walls. In 3D, U = (U₁, U₂) gives the two tangential components in increasing axis order. Each value is a number or a function of the coordinates along the wall.

source
CFBoxes.CGrid.VortexEnergyCons — Type
VortexEnergyCons()

Energy-conserving vortex force (Sadourny 1975, eq. 3), in 2D and 3D: the potential vorticity η = ω/ρ and the mass flux are multiplied at the vertices (edges in 3D), then interpolated to the faces, e.g. (ω×u)_x = ℑz(η Wᴵ). The force does no work.

source
CFBoxes.CGrid.VortexEnergyEnstrophyCons — Type
VortexEnergyEnstrophyCons()

Energy- and potential-enstrophy-conserving vortex force (Arakawa & Lamb 1981, eqs 3.5, 3.6 and 3.34), in 2D only: each mass flux on a face of a cell is weighted by a combination of the four η = ω/ρ around that cell, with extra terms along the flux direction ("unphysical Coriolis" terms). The force does no work, and conserves Σ ρ η²/2 for any mass flux.

source
CFBoxes.CGrid.VortexEnstrophyCons — Type
VortexEnstrophyCons()

Enstrophy-conserving vortex force (Sadourny 1975, eq. 4), in 2D only: η = ω/ρ and the mass flux are interpolated to the faces separately, then multiplied, e.g. (ω×u)_x = ℑz(η) ℑz(Wᴵ). It conserves the potential enstrophy Σ ρ η²/2 of the advection, but not the energy.

source
CFBoxes.CGrid.VortexScheme — Type
VortexScheme

Discretization of the vortex force ω×u (see vortex_force!): VortexEnergyCons(), VortexEnstrophyCons() or VortexEnergyEnstrophyCons().

source
CFBoxes.CGrid.Wall — Type
Wall(; tangential, tracers)

Impermeable wall with a tangential momentum condition (FreeSlip(), NoSlip(), TangentialVelocity(U) or Stress(τ)) and one condition per tracer (Flux(F), HeatFlux(F) or Value(c)), in the order in which the model stores its tracers, e.g. tracers = (Value(300.0), Flux(0.0)) for (consvar, q).

source
CFBoxes.CGrid.ZeroGradient — Type
ZeroGradient()

Ghost rule for fill_halo!: each ghost equals its mirror image in the fluid, so that every difference across a wall vanishes.

source
CFBoxes.CGrid.HeatFlux — Method
HeatFlux(F)

Prescribed heat flux F into the fluid [W m⁻²], for the first tracer (the conservative variable) only. The model divides it by the conjugate variable of the cell next to the wall to get the flux of ρ consvar, so that the energy input is exactly F.

CFBoxes.CGrid.alloc — Method
alloc(F::Type, mgr, box, location::Tuple, dims...) -> Array

Zero array of element type F on the grid location: one Face() or Cell() per dimension, e.g. (Cell(), Cell()) for the cells, (Face(), Cell()) the u-faces, (Cell(), Face()) the w-faces and (Face(), Face()) the vertices of a 2D box. Trailing dims add extra dimensions (e.g. tracers). Starting from zero defines the halo points that no kernel writes (e.g. beyond walls).

The loop manager mgr writes the zeros (nothing: serially), its threads sharing out the last box dimension as in the kernels, so that on a multi-socket node each thread's memory pages are placed on its own socket.

CFBoxes.CGrid.vortex_force! — Method
vortex_x, vortex_z, scratch = vortex_force!((vortex_x, vortex_z, scratch), model, scheme::VortexScheme, (u, w), ρ)

2D vortex force ω×u [m s⁻²]: vortex_x = (ω×u)x = ω w on the u-faces and `vortexz= (ω×u)_z = -ω u on the w-faces, with ω = ∂u/∂z - ∂w/∂x at the vertices, the velocity(u, w)on the faces and the densityρat the cells: a cell array, or a vertical profile (a vector, e.g. the anelastic reference density). The momentum tendencies are-vortexxand-vortexz.schemeisVortexEnergyCons()orVortexEnstrophyCons()(Sadourny 1975, eqs 3 and 4), orVortexEnergyEnstrophyCons()` (Arakawa & Lamb 1981).

The outputs and scratch (a tuple of intermediate arrays, which depends on the scheme) are reused when given as arrays, and allocated when given as void. The first arrays of scratch are the face densities, (ρᴵ, ρᴶ) in 2D and (ρᴵ, ρᴶ, ρᴷ) in 3D. model has the fields mgr, domain, spatial_scheme, inv_dx and inv_dz (and inv_dy in 3D).

vortex_x, vortex_y, vortex_z, scratch = vortex_force!((vortex_x, vortex_y, vortex_z, scratch), model,
                                                      VortexEnergyCons(), (u, v, w), ρ)

3D vortex force ω×u on the u-, v- and w-faces, with ω = ∇×u on the edges (energy-conserving only: enstrophy is not an invariant of 3D flow).

CFBoxes.CGrid.vortex_faces! — Method
vortex_x, vortex_z = vortex_faces!((vortex_x, vortex_z), model, scheme, (η, Uᴶ, Wᴵ))

The 2D vortex force on the faces from η and the mass fluxes at the vertices. VortexEnergyCons() (Sadourny 1975, eq. 3) interpolates the products at the vertices, vortex_x = ℑz(η Wᴵ), vortex_z = -ℑx(η Uᴶ); VortexEnstrophyCons() (eq. 4) multiplies the interpolations, vortex_x = ℑz(η) ℑz(Wᴵ), vortex_z = -ℑx(η) ℑx(Uᴶ).
